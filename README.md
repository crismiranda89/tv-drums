# Bateria TV

3D rhythm drum game for the phone and the TV: hi-hat, snare and floor tom, notes fall onto the drums, hit them on the beat to build combo and multiplier.

- **TV (pointer remote / air mouse):** the stick follows the pointer, rising from below the screen. Lift it, then drop it onto the drum as the ring lands.
- **Phone:** tap the drums.
- Runs on Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) TV browsers; graphics lower themselves on slower devices.

Play: https://crismiranda89.github.io/tv-drums/ · Old free-play kit: https://crismiranda89.github.io/tv-drums/classic.html

## Pulse Rail

Neon rhythm rail shooter: the city moves to the music, shoot enemies on the beat with the pointer remote (pistol or kid-friendly shotgun).

Play: https://crismiranda89.github.io/tv-drums/pulserail/ opens a "Who's playing?" page: **Kids** goes to Pulse Rail Kids, **Grown-ups** asks a quiz PIN (the meaning of the universe) and then opens the game (`pulserail/play.html`, currently v9). The PIN only keeps small children out; it is not security. Earlier versions: `pulserail/pulserail-v1.html` … `pulserail-v10.html`. Record plays (Red button or R) sends a performance log to Jarvis on the home network; if the TV cannot reach it, "Send log by phone" shows two QR codes to scan: 1 = summary of the whole play, 2 = every frame from the start of the recording until the code is full (a few seconds).

**Pulse Rail Kids** — the same game re-dressed for small children (sunny candy town, rainbow arches, round Boppers that throw soap bubbles, confetti pops, happy songs, no way to lose). Play: https://crismiranda89.github.io/tv-drums/pulserail-kids/
